About Us

Independent Care, Family Spirit.

Peekaboo Vision is more than just an optical dispensary—we are a locally owned practice run by five partners who share a single goal: providing exceptional care to our neighbours.

As an independent dispensary, we have the freedom to curate a unique selection of high-quality eyewear and spend the time necessary to get your prescription exactly right. We’ve built our practice on a foundation of trust and community, creating an environment where every member of your family feels at home. Whether it’s a child’s first pair of glasses or a complex lens fitting, you’ll always find a friendly face and expert advice ready to help you see your best.
